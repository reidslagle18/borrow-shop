# Editing your marketing pages in Plasmic

A one-page guide to editing **borrowfayetteville.com** marketing pages yourself — no code, no prompting.

## What you can edit
- **Home**, **About**, **FAQ**, **Size guide**, **Lookbook**

Everything else — the shop, product pages, checkout, reservations, your account,
the consignor portal, and the admin studio — stays in code and is **not** editable
here, on purpose.

## What you can change on those pages
Text, photos, buttons/links, the order of sections, spacing, and which on-brand
background a section uses. Your colors and fonts are locked, so edits always stay
on brand.

---

## 1. Log in
1. Go to **plasmic.app** and sign in.
2. Open the **Borrow Marketing** project.

## 2. Edit text
- Double-click any text and type. That's it.
- Use the right-hand panel for size/weight if needed (fonts stay Cormorant for
  headings, Karla for body — you can't pick an off-brand font).

## 3. Change a photo
- Click an image → in the right panel click the image thumbnail → **Upload** a new
  one (or pick from recent). Use a nice high-res photo; it will crop to fit.

## 4. Move or reorder sections
- Click a section once to select the whole block (the breadcrumb at the top-left
  shows what's selected).
- Drag it up or down in the **left "Outline" tree**, or drag on the canvas.
- To add a section: click the blue **＋** (top-left) → under **"Borrow sections"**
  pick **Hero, Photo + text, Product row, FAQ,** or **Announcement banner** →
  drag it where you want it.

## 5. Buttons & links
- Click a button → in the right panel set its **label** and its **link**
  (e.g. `/shop`, `/dropoff`, `/lookbook`).

## 6. Preview on a phone
- Top bar: click the **device / responsive** icon and choose a phone width, or
  click the **▷ (preview)** button and resize your browser window.
- All Borrow sections are built mobile-first, so they stack neatly on phones.

## 7. Publish (make it live)
- Click **Publish** (top-right) → **Publish**.
- Your change goes live within ~5 minutes (the site refreshes published content
  automatically; no deploy needed).

## 8. Undo / roll back
- While editing: **Cmd/Ctrl + Z**.
- To roll back a published change: **Publish** button → **See published versions**
  → right-click an earlier version → **Revert to this version**, then **Publish**
  again.

---

## Good to know
- **If Plasmic is ever down or slow:** your pages keep working. The site serves
  the last published version, and if a page was never published in Plasmic it
  shows the original coded version. Shoppers never see a broken page.
- **Your brand is locked:** section backgrounds are limited to Borrow tones
  (white, soft pink, soft lavender, warm white, sage, yellow), and fonts are
  fixed. You can't accidentally go off-brand.
- **The "Product row" is live:** it pulls real pieces from your shop. Set it to
  **Featured** (follows your studio Featured list) or **Most loved**, and choose
  how many to show.
- **Need a new editable page** (beyond the five above)? That's a quick code change
  — just ask.
